Interpreter Chrome Extension

Use Interpreter with the tab you choose.

Open the site you want, then click the extension on that tab. Interpreter will ask before taking browser actions that need permission.

  1. 1
    Open Interpreter

    Keep the app running while you use the extension.

  2. 2
    Open the site you want to use

    Go to the page you want Interpreter to see and use.

  3. 3
    Click the extension on that tab

    That connects the current tab to Interpreter. Click it again to turn that same tab off.

If you do not see the button, pin it from Chrome's extensions menu.
