Interpreter Chrome Extension

Click the extension on the tab you want.

Keep Interpreter open. Each click only affects the tab you are on.

  1. 1
    Open Interpreter

    Keep the app running while you use the extension.

  2. 2
    Open the site you want

    Go to the page you want Interpreter to use.

  3. 3
    Click the extension on that tab

    That turns the current tab on. Click it again to turn that same tab off.

If you do not see the button, pin it from Chrome's extensions menu.
